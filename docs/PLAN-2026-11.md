# Plan: Newsletter hub, 3D story homepage, PDFs, references (Nov 2026)

Decisions confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-28:

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Where does the 3D story live? | It **replaces the homepage**. Existing proof sections become chapters of the journey. |
| Subscribe | The site is the **standalone home for every post**; LinkedIn is distribution. Add a **Follow on LinkedIn** button. |
| 360-question PDF | **Both editions**: Study (answers inline) and Practice (answer key at the back). |
| Content location | Top-level **`content/`** folder. |

## Phases

1. **Repo layout.** Move `src/content/` → `content/`. Add `HOW-TO-PUBLISH.md` (upload a newsletter issue from
   github.com in five steps) and `content/README.md` (map of every folder).
2. **Newsletter.** Rename Blog → Newsletter: `/newsletter`, `/newsletter/<slug>`, `/newsletter/topics/<topic>`,
   `/rss.xml`; old `/blog/*` URLs redirect. Masthead, numbered issues, topic filters (plain labels), reading layout,
   share bar, next/previous, Follow on LinkedIn. SEO: `seoTitle`, per-issue OG image from the cover,
   `BlogPosting` JSON-LD, sitemap, RSS. Remove "I Built a Financial AI That Refuses to Guess". Publish
   "How to Register for a Claude Certification Exam" as Issue #1 with its cover and screenshots.
3. **References page.** `/references`: LinkedIn posts and articles from `content/references/links.md`. Filters by
   source and topic, search, cards that open the original.
4. **PDFs.** `scripts/build-claude-pdf.mjs` renders both editions from the same Markdown the site uses. A
   verification step re-reads the PDFs and checks every question ID, correct letter, and explanation is present
   (faithfulness, groundedness). Download buttons on Claude Prep and the review page.
5. **3D story homepage ("Mission Log").** Three.js scene (bundled locally, no CDN): starfield, a ship, a planet per
   chapter, camera flight tied to scroll. Chapters carry real content: Launch (intro + quote), Credentials, Impact,
   How I work, Projects, Newsletter, Claude Prep, Experience, Contact. Light mode = dawn sky, dark mode = deep space.
   Mobile: lower pixel ratio, fewer stars. Fallback: static gradient sky when WebGL is missing or reduced motion is on.
6. **Fixes.** Remove 50K+ from the dashboard. Guinness certificate photo on its credential card (tap to enlarge) and
   on About. Readability pass.
7. **Test and ship.** Audit at 375/820/1440 in light and dark, interaction tests, PDF verification, deploy, verify live.
